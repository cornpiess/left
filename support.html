常见问题解答 (FAQ)

Q1: “毛球”会上传我的照片到服务器吗？

答：不会。“毛球”采用 100% 本地分析引擎，所有照片读取、整理与删除均在您的 iPhone 本地运行，绝不上传任何照片数据。

Q2: 误删的照片如何找回？

答：在“毛球”中点击移入待删相册的照片，可随时在【待删相册】页面点击【恢复】。若已在待删相册中彻底确认清理，也可在 iPhone 的【照片】App ➔【最近删除】相册中 30 天内恢复。

Q3: 换了新手机后如何恢复 PRO 会员？

答：只需在应用右上角菜单或会员弹窗中，点击【恢复购买】按钮，系统会自动识别您的 Apple ID 并恢复您的 PRO 权益。

LeftSwipe - Help & Support

Welcome to the LeftSwipe Support Center. If you have any questions, encounter issues, or have feature suggestions, we are always here to help.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Does LeftSwipe upload my photos to any server?

Answer: No. LeftSwipe is powered by a 100% on-device processing engine. All photo browsing, organizing, and deletion take place strictly locally on your iPhone. Your media never leaves your device.

Q2: How can I recover accidentally deleted photos?

Answer: Photos moved to the Review Bin can be restored at any time prior to final confirmation by tapping "Restore". Even after you confirm deletion in LeftSwipe, iOS moves them to the "Recently Deleted" album in Apple Photos, where you can still recover them within 30 days.

Q3: How do I restore my PRO membership on a new device?

Answer: Simply open the top-right menu or any membership prompt in the app, and tap "Restore Purchases". Apple StoreKit will verify your Apple ID and instantly restore your PRO access.
